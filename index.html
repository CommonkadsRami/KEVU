Most AI programmes stall between the demo and production.

The tech isn't usually the blocker. Scattered tooling, no owner after handover, and pilots that never earn their keep are what quietly kill the ROI.

Your team juggles a dozen disconnected tools daily. The same copy-paste, re-key and reconcile loop runs every week and never compounds.
